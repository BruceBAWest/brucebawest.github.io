    TMF Calls for AI Project Proposals to Drive Technology Modernization; Clare Martorana Emphasizes Opportunity¶

    AI Project Proposals Sought by the Technology Modernization Fund¶

    The Technology Modernization Fund (TMF) is soliciting proposals for artificial intelligence (AI) projects. These projects are intended to assist federal agencies in modernizing systems, improving service delivery, and hastening mission objectives.

    Invitation for AI Proposals¶

    TMF’s call for project proposals aims to advance an October 2023 executive order that mandates the secure, reliable development and usage of AI technology across federal government operations.

    TMF’s Focus Areas¶

    In a distinct announcement, the TMF expressed its interest in project proposals that will expedite AI deployment, facilitate process automation, enable assistive technology, augment customer experiences, and lessen administrative overhead.

    Acceleration of AI Integration in Federal Systems¶

    Proposals that align with the executive order and the TMF’s announced focus areas stand at the intersection of AI and governmental modernization. They represent an exciting opportunity to leverage AI for optimizing various aspects of governmental operations.

    Conclusion¶

    TMF’s call for AI project proposals represents a strategic move towards harnessing AI’s transformative potential in government services. Consolidating efforts to promote responsible AI use is a big step towards advancing national AI strategies. More on TMF’s announcement can be found in the original Executive Gov article .
