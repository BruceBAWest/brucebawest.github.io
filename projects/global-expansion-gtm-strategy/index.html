Growth + market strategy

Global Expansion Go-to-Market Strategy

Market expansion is not a geography exercise. It is a coordinated decision system spanning customer insight, positioning, economics, channels, partnerships, execution, and learning.

Rice University client engagement2020–2021Implemented commercial strategy
Result / value signalClient implemented the plan and launched in ANZ and Singapore

Context

A flagship consumer-technology product needed a credible growth path into Australia, New Zealand, and broader Asia-Pacific markets. The opportunity required more than market sizing: product meaning, pricing, distribution, retail, e-commerce, and launch execution had to align.

Mandate

Partner with the client's executive sponsor to build an actionable market-entry and brand strategy that could move from analysis into launch decisions.

What I led

  • Conducted consumer, market, competitor, and financial analysis to identify viable entry points and differentiated positioning.
  • Developed segmentation, pricing architecture, TAM/SAM, retail and e-commerce channel strategy, and partnership recommendations.
  • Translated the strategic narrative into creative-brief guidance, launch metrics, and an implementation roadmap.
  • Integrated brand, channel, customer-experience, and commercial-economics decisions into one launch system.

Outcomes

  • Delivered a complete market-entry and localized brand strategy for ANZ/APAC expansion.
  • Provided the positioning, channel, partnership, and measurement architecture required for execution.
  • The client implemented the plan and launched the product line in Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore.

Leadership signal

Shows commercial strategy range beyond technical delivery: synthesizing research, economics, brand, channels, and execution into decisions an executive team can act on.
