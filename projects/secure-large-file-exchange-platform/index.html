Cloud product + security

Secure Large-File Exchange Platform

Secure exchange at enterprise scale requires product, architecture, operational resilience, and authorization evidence to advance together—especially when inherited prototypes are not production ready.

Federal delivery engagement2022–2023Production deployment · authorization achieved
Result / value signalPrototype to production and Authority to Operate in six months

Context

An inherited prototype had significant architectural and functional deficiencies but needed to become a reliable production capability for large-file transfer under stringent federal and intelligence-community requirements.

Mandate

Lead the redesign, cloud architecture, security integration, operational hardening, accreditation, and launch of a platform capable of handling files up to terabyte scale.

What I led

  • Directed end-to-end product and AWS architecture redesign around scalability, security, reliability, and operational support.
  • Integrated authenticated access, zero-trust principles, virus scanning, error resilience, and secure data-management controls.
  • Aligned engineering delivery with the evidence and documentation required for authorization.
  • Managed the compressed path from inherited prototype to accredited production service.

Outcomes

  • Completed the Authority to Operate process within a six-month delivery window.
  • Launched a secure, scalable, production-ready large-file exchange capability.
  • Resolved core deficiencies while meeting demanding security and operational criteria.

Leadership signal

Demonstrates accountable product delivery under severe constraints: inheriting ambiguity, rebuilding the architecture, aligning assurance with execution, and carrying the capability through production launch.
