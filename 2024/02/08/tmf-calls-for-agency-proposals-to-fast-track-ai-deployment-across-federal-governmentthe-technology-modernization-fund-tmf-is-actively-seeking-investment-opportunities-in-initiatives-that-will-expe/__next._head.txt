5:T4dc,TMF Calls for Agency Proposals to Fast-Track AI Deployment across Federal Government The Technology Modernization Fund (TMF) is actively seeking investment opportunities in initiatives that will expedite the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) resources throughout the federal government. The TMF’s program management office has extended an open invitation for agency proposals aimed at advancing the goals set out in President Joe Biden’s executive order. This order pertains to the development of AI in the governmental sector that is “safe, secure, and trustworthy.” The General Services Administration, the home of the TMF PMO, announced that TMF board would prioritize reviews for proposals that request up to $6 million in funding and target projects with a completion timeline of 1.5 years or less. This strategy is a concerted effort to boost the government’s AI usage and unlock innovative potential. Federal agencies have a unique opportunity to contribute to the national effort of integrating AI tools across government operations. By catalyzing AI deployment, TMF seeks to enable rapid, responsive service delivery, enhancing both customer experience and the efficiency of administrative tasks. | Bruce B. A.
