5:T414,DHS Launches AI Corps, Calls for 50 AI Experts to Drive Tech Transformation The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is initiating a “hiring sprint” to assemble a team of 50 artificial intelligence (AI) experts for its newly established AI Corps. The AI Corps, modeled after the U.S. Digital Service, aims to leverage AI technology across various DHS operations. Experts in the AI Corps will contribute to countering illegal drug activities, combating child exploitation, providing immigration services, ensuring national security, protecting critical infrastructure, and enhancing cybersecurity. This move aligns with President Joe Biden’s AI executive order and demonstrates the government’s commitment to hiring AI talent to drive technological advancements in the federal sector. DHS plans to expedite the hiring process using flexible authorities to secure qualified candidates promptly. The goal is to responsibly harness AI capabilities, mitigate misuse, and advance the critical mission of homeland security. | Bruce B. A.
