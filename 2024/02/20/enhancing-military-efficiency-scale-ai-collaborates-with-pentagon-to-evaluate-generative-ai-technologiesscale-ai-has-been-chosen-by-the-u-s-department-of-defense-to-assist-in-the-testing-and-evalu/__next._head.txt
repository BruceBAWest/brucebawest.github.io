5:T48e,Enhancing Military Efficiency: Scale AI Collaborates with Pentagon to Evaluate Generative AI Technologies Scale AI has been chosen by the U.S. Department of Defense to assist in the testing and evaluation of generative artificial intelligence for military applications. This partnership, announced by the California-based company, will focus on producing benchmarks for large language models, a subtype of generative AI that can generate text, images, and more based on statistical relationships among input data. The Defense Department’s interest in generative AI has been increasing, although its potential applications and risks are subject to ongoing discussions. Scale AI’s founder, Alexandr Wang, highlighted the importance of adopting secure and verifiable AI models to address issues like misinformation, bias, and data breaches. By leveraging Scale’s test-and-evaluation procedures, the DOD aims to grasp the capabilities and limitations of generative AI technologies to ensure responsible deployment. This strategic collaboration underscores the significance of data quality and innovation in enhancing AI systems for military use. | Bruce B. A.
